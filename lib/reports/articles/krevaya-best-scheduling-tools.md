# The best tools to schedule social media posts

*Every scheduler can put a post on a calendar.*
*The differences are in what you pay and who gets to press publish.*

We built Krevaya, so weigh our place on this list accordingly. We checked each tool's pricing page in November 2026, kept the comparison to the same few questions, and gave every product, ours too, one honest catch.

**Key takeaways**
- Price models differ more than features: per channel, per user, or per plan.
- Buffer and Publer suit solo creators with a handful of accounts.
- Hootsuite charges per user and lists approvals only on Advanced.
- Postiz is the open-source pick; Metricool is the analytics pick.
- Krevaya fits teams that review every post and let AI agents draft.

## How to choose in five minutes

Start with one number: how many accounts you post to. Then count the people who need to log in. **Those two numbers decide the price** far more than any feature on a comparison page.

A solo creator with four accounts should look at per-channel or free plans. A team of three with fifteen accounts should avoid per-seat and per-channel pricing and look for a flat plan. Agencies need workspaces per client.

> The cheapest scheduler for three accounts is rarely the cheapest for thirty.

After price, ask who approves posts before they go live. Some tools put approvals in every paid plan. Others hold them back for the expensive tier. If a second pair of eyes matters to you, check that row first.

### Buffer

Buffer's free plan covers three channels, and Essentials costs $6 per channel per month on monthly billing, per [Buffer's pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing). It's the easiest tool here to learn. Pick it for a few channels. Past ten, the per-channel bill starts to sting.

### Later

Later's Starter plan is $18.75 a month on yearly billing for one social set of eight profiles, per [Later's pricing](https://later.com/pricing-v3). Pick it if Instagram is your main channel and you plan visually. Starter limits each profile to 30 posts a month.

### Publer

Publer is free for up to three accounts (X excluded), and Professional starts at $5 a month for one account, per [Publer's plans article](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh). Pick it if every dollar counts. Every tenth account is free, which helps as you grow.

### Metricool

Metricool's free plan covers one brand and 20 scheduled posts a month, and Starter is $25 a month for up to five brands, per [Metricool's pricing](https://metricool.com/pricing/). Pick it if you send clients monthly reports and want the scheduler in the same place.

### SocialBee

SocialBee's Bootstrap plan is $29 a month for five profiles, per [SocialBee's pricing](https://socialbee.com/pricing). It sorts posts into categories that recycle on a schedule. Great for evergreen content. Plans below Pro include one user.

### Postiz

Postiz is open source, and its hosted Standard plan is $29 a month for five channels, per [Postiz pricing](https://postiz.com/pricing). Pick it if you'd rather self-host, or want the code. Expect to trade some polish for that control.

### Hootsuite

Hootsuite Standard is $99 per user per month on annual billing for ten accounts, and approval workflows are listed only from Advanced at $399, per [Hootsuite's pricing](https://hootsuite.com/pricing). Pick it if you need enterprise listening and compliance. For a small team, it's the most expensive way to schedule.

## What none of these comparisons tell you

Feature tables flatten the thing that matters day to day: how a post gets from idea to published. And that changes by team.

So try two tools for a week with your real accounts. Schedule the same ten posts in each. Notice where you hesitate, what you have to click twice, and whether the person who approves posts actually uses the tool or asks for screenshots in chat. That last one is the tell.

## FAQ

### What is the best free tool to schedule social media posts?

For most people, Buffer or Publer. Buffer's free plan covers three channels with ten queued posts each, and Publer's covers three accounts, excluding X. Metricool's free plan covers one brand with 20 scheduled posts a month, and Krevaya's covers one account and five posts. Pick the free plan that includes the networks you actually use.

### Can I schedule posts to Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok from one tool?

Yes. Every tool on this list schedules to several networks from one calendar, but coverage differs in the details, such as LinkedIn company pages, video posts or X support on free plans. Check each tool's list of supported channels for the exact networks and post types you need before you commit.

### How much should a small team pay for a social media scheduler?

For most small teams, a flat plan between $29 and $99 a month covers it: Krevaya Plus, Postiz Team and SocialBee Pro all sit in that range. Per-channel and per-user pricing can push past it quickly as you add accounts or people, so price your real numbers before choosing.

### Do social media schedulers post automatically?

Yes. Once a post is scheduled, the tool publishes it at the set time through its connection to the network, and you don't need to be online. Some tools, including Krevaya, can hold each post for approval first, so nothing publishes until a teammate has checked it.

# The best Buffer alternatives in 2026

*Buffer bills by the channel.*
*That's fine at three accounts and painful at twelve.*

We built Krevaya, so weigh our place on this list accordingly. We checked every price below on the vendor's own pricing page in November 2026, and every rival gets a real reason to pick it, including over us.

**Key takeaways**
- Buffer's paid plans charge per channel, so cost climbs with every account you add.
- Flat-priced tools win once you manage roughly eight or more channels.
- Publer and Metricool have the most useful free plans for testing.
- Postiz is the pick if you want open source you can self-host.
- Krevaya fits teams that want a review queue and AI agents posting through it.

## Why people leave Buffer

The short answer is **per-channel pricing**. Buffer's Essentials plan costs $6 per channel per month on monthly billing, and Team costs $12 per channel, according to [Buffer's pricing page](https://buffer.com/pricing) as of November 2026. So a team running ten channels on the Team plan pays about $120 a month before any discount.

That math is the whole story for most switchers. Buffer itself is good. The composer is calm, the free plan covers three channels, and Team adds approvals and unlimited members. But every new client, brand or network is another line on the bill, and that's what pushes small teams to look around.

> At ten channels, Buffer's Team plan costs more than most flat-priced tools charge for twenty-five.

There's a second reason, quieter but real. Buffer's free plan caps each channel at 10 scheduled posts at a time, which works for testing and not for a content calendar a month deep.

### 2. Publer

Pick Publer if you're one person watching every dollar. Its free plan covers three social accounts (X excluded) and the Professional plan starts at $5 a month for one account, per [Publer's plans article](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh) updated September 2026. Every tenth account is free, which softens the curve.

But it still scales per account, so a growing agency ends up doing the same arithmetic it did on Buffer.

### 3. SocialBee

SocialBee is built around **content categories that recycle**. Bootstrap costs $29 a month for five profiles and Pro costs $99 for twenty-five, per [SocialBee's pricing](https://socialbee.com/pricing). Pick it if most of your posts are evergreen and you want them to rotate on their own.

The trade-off is seats. Bootstrap and Accelerate include one user, so teams land on Pro sooner than they'd like.

### 4. Postiz

Postiz is open source, and you can self-host it. The hosted Standard plan is $29 a month for five channels and Team is $39 for ten channels with unlimited members, according to [Postiz pricing](https://postiz.com/pricing). Pick it if owning your stack matters more than polish.

Self-hosting saves the subscription, not the time. Someone has to keep it running.

### 5. Metricool

Metricool is an analytics tool that schedules, not the other way around. The free plan covers one brand and 20 scheduled posts a month, and Starter is $25 a month for up to five brands, per [Metricool's pricing](https://metricool.com/pricing/). Pick it if reports for clients matter as much as the calendar.

### 6. Later

Later still feels made for Instagram. Starter is $18.75 a month on yearly billing with one social set of eight profiles, per [Later's pricing](https://later.com/pricing-v3). Pick it for visual planning. Watch the 30 posts per profile each month on Starter (it adds up fast if you post daily).

## When to stay on Buffer

Stay if you run three channels or fewer. Seriously.

At that size, per-channel pricing is cheap and Buffer's free plan may be all you need. Stay too if your team already lives in Buffer's approval flow and nobody wants to retrain. Switching costs time, and a small monthly saving isn't worth a lost week.

Switch when the channel count, not the feature list, is what hurts. That's the point where a flat plan pays for the migration within a month or two.

If agents drafting posts into a review queue sounds like your team, [Krevaya](https://krevaya.com/) is worth joining the waitlist for, and the [Buffer comparison](https://krevaya.com/vs/buffer/) has the full feature table.

## FAQ

### Is there a free alternative to Buffer?

Yes. Publer's free plan covers three social accounts excluding X, and Metricool's free plan covers one brand with 20 scheduled posts a month. Krevaya also has a free plan for one account and five posts a month. None of them replace a paid plan for a team, but each is enough to test the workflow before you pay anything or move your calendar over.

### Why is Buffer getting expensive?

Buffer charges per channel. Essentials is $6 per channel per month and Team is $12 on monthly billing, so the bill grows with every account you connect. Volume discounts start above ten channels. For a small team with many brands or clients, that structure costs more than flat-priced tools that include twenty or more accounts in one price.

### What is the best Buffer alternative for agencies?

It depends on seats versus accounts. SocialBee's Pro plan includes 25 profiles and several users per workspace. Postiz Team includes ten channels with unlimited members. Krevaya Plus lists 25 accounts and adds a review queue for client approval. Price out your real channel count on two of them before deciding, because the cheapest option changes as you grow.

### Can I move my scheduled posts from Buffer?

Usually not automatically. Most tools, including the ones here, ask you to reconnect your social accounts and rebuild or import your queue. Plan the switch at the start of a week, export anything you'll need from Buffer's analytics first, and keep both tools running for a few days so nothing scheduled falls through the gap.
